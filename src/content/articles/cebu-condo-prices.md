# Cebu Condo Prices 2026: Complete Guide to Condominium Costs in Cebu

If you are planning to purchase a condominium, understanding current Cebu condo prices is an important first step.

Cebu has a diverse condominium market, offering affordable studio units, modern one-bedroom apartments, spacious family condominiums, and premium residential developments.

Whether you are a first-time homebuyer, an overseas Filipino worker (OFW), or a real estate investor, knowing how much condominiums cost in different locations can help you identify properties that match your budget.

Prices vary depending on the location, developer, building quality, floor area, amenities, and payment arrangements.

In this guide, we explore condominium prices in Cebu, compare different property types and locations, and explain what buyers should consider before making a purchase.

## How Much Are Condos in Cebu in 2026?

Condominium prices in Cebu vary significantly depending on the property type and location.

Recent property listing data shows a wide range of asking prices, from below ₱1 million for selected resale or distressed listings to premium condominiums worth tens of millions of pesos.

A November 2026 listing-market snapshot from Filipino Homes reports a median asking price of approximately ₱4.2 million across its Cebu listings. Its reported median price per square meter is approximately ₱137,000.

These are listing-market figures, not official transaction prices or guaranteed property valuations.

Source: Filipino Homes Cebu condominium listings.

## What Affects Condominium Prices in Cebu?

Before comparing properties, understand the factors that influence their selling prices.

### 1. Location

Properties near major business districts and commercial establishments may command higher prices.

Accessibility to workplaces, schools, hospitals, and shopping centers can also influence buyer demand.

### 2. Floor Area

Larger condominium units generally have higher total prices.

However, smaller units in premium locations may have higher prices per square meter.

### 3. Developer Reputation

The developer's experience, construction quality, project delivery record, and property management arrangements are important considerations.

### 4. Building Amenities

Condominiums with swimming pools, gyms, security systems, landscaped areas, and other facilities may have higher acquisition and maintenance costs.

### 5. Property Condition

Ready-for-occupancy and resale units may differ significantly in price depending on age, maintenance, furnishing, and renovation requirements.

### 6. View and Floor Level

Units with attractive views, higher floors, corner positions, or balconies may carry additional price premiums.

## Pre-Selling vs. Ready-for-Occupancy Condo Prices

Buyers can choose between pre-selling developments and completed condominium units.

### Pre-Selling Condominiums

Pre-selling properties are sold before project completion.

Potential advantages include:

- Flexible installment arrangements
- Early access to selected units
- Developer payment promotions
- Potentially longer payment periods

However, buyers must consider construction delays, delivery risks, changing market conditions, and future rental competition.

Always verify the developer's License to Sell and relevant project approvals.

### Ready-for-Occupancy Condominiums

Ready-for-occupancy properties are completed units that may be available for immediate use, subject to the transaction and turnover requirements.

Advantages include:

- Ability to inspect the actual unit
- Existing building facilities
- Potentially earlier rental income
- More reliable assessment of the surrounding community

Compare the total purchase cost rather than assuming one category is automatically cheaper.

## How to Calculate the Price Per Square Meter

Price per square meter is a useful way to compare condominium properties.

The formula is:

**Price per sqm = Total Property Price ÷ Floor Area**

Example:

| Property | Price | Floor Area | Price per sqm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Condo A | ₱4,000,000 | 25 sqm | ₱160,000 |
| Condo B | ₱5,000,000 | 30 sqm | ₱166,667 |
| Condo C | ₱7,000,000 | 35 sqm | ₱200,000 |

Although Condo C has the largest price, it also has a different cost per square meter.

When comparing units, make sure the floor-area measurement and price basis are consistent.

Check whether the advertised price includes VAT, parking, balcony area, and other charges.

## Additional Expenses When Buying a Condo in Cebu

The advertised condominium price is not necessarily your complete acquisition cost.

Prepare for additional expenses such as:

- Documentary Stamp Tax
- Transfer Tax
- Registration fees
- Notarial fees
- Capital Gains Tax, where applicable
- Loan processing expenses
- Insurance
- Condominium association dues
- Utility deposits
- Furnishing and renovation expenses

The allocation of transaction expenses should be clearly stated in the contract.

Request a complete computation from the seller or developer before making a reservation.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Cebu Condo Prices

### How much is the cheapest condo in Cebu?

Selected resale and smaller condominium listings may be available below ₱3 million. However, condition, location, unit size, ownership documentation, and availability must be checked carefully.

### How much is a studio condo in Cebu?

For initial budgeting, consider approximately ₱2 million to ₱5 million for many entry-level options. Premium locations and developments may cost more.

### How much is a one-bedroom condo in Cebu?

A broad planning budget is approximately ₱3.5 million to ₱7 million, although premium units may exceed this range.

### How much is a two-bedroom condo in Cebu?

Many buyers should initially budget approximately ₱4.5 million to ₱12 million. Larger and premium units can cost significantly more.

### Is Cebu IT Park expensive for condominium buyers?

Cebu IT Park generally commands a location premium because of its established business environment and residential demand. Buyers can compare resale units and new developments to find suitable options.

### Is Mactan cheaper than Cebu City?

Some residential developments in Mactan have lower entry prices than premium Cebu City developments. However, luxury resort-style properties in Mactan can be substantially more expensive.

### Are condominium prices negotiable in Cebu?

Some resale properties may have room for negotiation. Developer promotions and payment arrangements may also affect the final acquisition cost.

### Is buying a condo in Cebu a good investment?

It may be suitable for buyers seeking rental income or long-term property ownership. However, investment performance depends on purchase price, rental demand, vacancy, operating expenses, and resale conditions.
